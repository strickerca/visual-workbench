## Goal

Get images into and out of both devices the ways Christian already works — especially straight into Claude Code.

## Steps

1. Android import: share target (image/*), photo picker, and the system camera. Each copies into the project as a content-hashed asset; large files are streamed; imports over 50 MP are refused with a clear message.
2. PC import: drag-and-drop, File > Open, and pasting an image from the clipboard (PNG or DIB); the same 50 MP limit.
3. Blob sync: assets transfer to the peer over the BLOB channel (chunked, hash-verified, resumable).
4. Export sheet on both devices: clean or marked × full or region (for now, region = the current selection rectangle or view rectangle); format choice; preflight messages; revision and resolution shown.
5. PC clipboard: through vw-host-win, set PNG (registered "PNG" format) plus CF_DIBV5. File drag-out uses a temporary PNG in `%TEMP%\VisualWorkbench` that is cleaned up later.
6. Android share: a FileProvider PNG; text is not attached (text staging comes in Phase 2).
7. Destination tests, with guards:
   - Before any scripted paste, tell Christian his clipboard will be replaced. Check that the foreground window is the intended app, send Ctrl+V only, and never press Enter or Send. Crop screenshots to the message box.
   - Targets: Claude desktop, ChatGPT desktop, and a browser chat (claude.ai or chatgpt.com in Edge).
   - Claude Code: you can't paste into your own session. Ask Christian to open a separate terminal, run `claude`, paste with Alt+V (or drag the file in), and tell you whether the image arrived.
   Record each result with a screenshot.
8. CORE-006: confirm that the capture-info tests from T1.02 and T1.03 pass, and that every export format carries revision metadata.
9. HOOK-001: import a short generated `.mp4` on one device; it syncs to the other as an ordinary asset with an identical BLAKE3 hash (no timeline).
10. SYNC-001: in airplane mode, the phone opens a project, edits it, and exports a marked PNG through the share sheet.
11. UX-005: screenshot EXPORT READY on both devices; together with T1.10's screenshots of SYNCED, SYNCING, RECONNECTING and OFFLINE (n pending), every Phase 1 status word has been observed on both devices.
