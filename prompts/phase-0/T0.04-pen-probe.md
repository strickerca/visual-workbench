## Goal

Find out exactly what the S23 Ultra's S Pen reports, and record real pen traces that automated tests can replay for the rest of the project.

## Steps

1. Create `tools/pen-trace/probe-android`, a minimal Kotlin app that gives direct access to MotionEvent. It logs every event including historical samples:
   - action, tool type, x, y, pressure, size;
   - AXIS_TILT, AXIS_ORIENTATION, AXIS_DISTANCE;
   - buttonState, flags (including FLAG_CANCELED), and event time (nanoseconds where available);
   - source and device id;
   - hover events and key events.
2. Air Actions:
   - Add declarative REMOTE_ACTION meta-data mapping click, double click, the four swipes and both circles to distinct key codes, per the Samsung documentation. (The proprietary S Pen Remote SDK is not used.)
   - Log what arrives, and note any manual enabling needed (Settings → Advanced features → S Pen → Air actions).
3. Barrel button: record buttonState with Samsung Air Command on and off (Christian toggles the setting). Note whether pressing the button while hovering opens Air Command, and whether the app still receives it.
4. Rates: from fast strokes, compute the contact report rate (median sample interval); also compute the hover report rate.
5. Latency estimate: draw the latest sample with androidx.graphics front-buffered rendering. Compare input event time with frame presentation (Choreographer or frame metrics). Record the method and its limits.
6. Trace recorder: a screen that saves named traces as JSON (device info; capability flags; every sample with all fields; hover segments; button events) and lets you pull them with adb into `fixtures/traces/`. Ask Christian to record about 24 traces:
   - 5 straight lines (slow and fast)
   - 3 circles
   - 3 short handwritten words
   - 3 fast flicks
   - 2 hover-only passes
   - 2 strokes with his palm resting on the screen
   - 2 strokes with a button press mid-stroke
   - 4 tilt calibration strokes: 2 with the pen clearly leaning toward the right edge of the screen, 2 leaning toward the bottom edge (phone in portrait)
7. Replay harness: an androidx.test instrumentation test that loads a trace and injects it with `UiAutomation.injectInputEvent`. It must reproduce TOOL_TYPE_STYLUS, pressure, tilt, orientation, hover actions and buttonState. The probe records the replayed trace. Sample counts must match exactly, and every replayed event timestamp must be within 1 ms of the recorded one (report the maximum and p95 deviation).
8. Wire the replay into the `hil-test` entry point.
9. Thermal baseline (QUALITY-004): while Christian draws casually in the probe for 10 minutes, log the battery temperature (`dumpsys battery`) every 30 s; record start, maximum and end.
10. Write the capability table in `docs/evidence/T0.04.md`:
    - pressure: levels observed, smallest step;
    - hover: yes/no, with or without distance;
    - tilt and orientation: yes/no and range; from the calibration strokes, the sign of AXIS_ORIENTATION when the pen leans right and when it leans toward the bottom (needed for the §4.11 conversion);
    - contact and hover report rates;
    - barrel delivery path, eraser behavior;
    - Air Actions delivery per gesture;
    - Air Command interference;
    - the thermal baseline.
11. Update `docs/evidence/hardware-truth.md` with these facts. In REQUIREMENTS.json, add evidence notes to PEN-013, PEN-014 and QUALITY-004 (facts only; don't change their status).
